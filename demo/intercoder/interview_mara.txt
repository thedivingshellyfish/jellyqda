Interview Mara (fictional), project lead, 34 years

[00:00:05] I: How did you end up in your current job?
[00:00:12] P: After university I spent two years in a large corporation. Everything was very anonymous there, you were just a number in the HR department.
[00:01:02] I: And then?

[00:04:12] I: Are there downsides as well?
